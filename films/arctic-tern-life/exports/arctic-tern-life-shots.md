# The life of an Arctic tern: shot list

32 seconds, 17 shots, 120 bpm, 1080x1920 at 24 fps. Every pixel and every sound is computed in JavaScript.

| # | Time | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | 0.0–1.5 | An adult Arctic tern hovers over the sea at Sand Island, north-east Greenland, a sand eel in its blood-red bill. |
| 02 | 1.5–4.0 | Blueprint: a spark becomes an egg; the first cell divides 1, 2, 4, 8 and curls into an embryo facing the air cell. |
| 03 | 4.0–5.5 | The speckled egg in its shingle scrape cracks at the blunt end, and a grey downy chick pushes off the cap. |
| 04 | 5.5–8.0 | The parents bring four sand eels; the chick grows from a ball of down to a feathering youngster. |
| 05 | 8.0–9.5 | Blueprint: 13 grams to adult weight in about 20 days, pin feathers breaking through. |
| 06 | 9.5–11.5 | The fledgling sheds its last down and stretches its new pointed wings. |
| 07 | 11.5–13.0 | Blueprint: a flight feather grows rolled in a sheath with a blood supply, then splits it and unrolls. |
| 08 | 13.0–16.0 | Under the midnight sun, which circles the sky without setting, the young bird practises flapping. |
| 09 | 16.0–18.0 | First flight, at about three weeks old. |
| 10 | 18.0–19.5 | Blueprint: the wing's arm and hand bones, ten primaries, and the air flowing over a long, narrow wing. |
| 11 | 19.5–22.0 | A zoom 40x into one feather: barbs, barbules, and hooks that zip the vane shut. |
| 12 | 22.0–23.5 | Blueprint: the globe from June to December; the tern follows the daylight from the Arctic to the Antarctic. |
| 13 | 23.5–26.5 | Pulling back from the colony to the whole Atlantic: south via a North Atlantic stopover, splitting along Africa or Brazil, and home on an S-shaped track, 70,900 km a year. |
| 14 | 26.5–28.0 | Small loose flocks ride the winds south over the open ocean. |
| 15 | 28.0–29.5 | The southern summer on the Weddell Sea pack ice: winter plumage, moulting, five months, then north again. |
| 16 | 29.5–30.5 | Back home, a mate offers a fish at the scrape, and the camera snaps onto a new egg. |
| 17 | 30.5–32.0 | Blueprint: the egg again, "arctic tern", and the cycle begins again. |

Sources: Egevang et al. 2010 (PNAS), Fijn et al. 2013, Alerstam et al. 2019, BTO, Cornell All About Birds, Audubon, Østnes et al. 1997, Hedenström and Åkesson 2016.
